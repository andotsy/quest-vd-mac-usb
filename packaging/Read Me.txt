Meta Quest Virtual Display USB

1. Run Install.command.
2. Open Meta Quest Virtual Display in Applications.
3. Allow Accessibility and Screen Recording. Restart when asked.
4. Plug in your Quest and choose your Mac’s USB connection.

The installer signs the app locally and keeps a backup of your previous copy.
No Xcode needed. If macOS blocks opening it, use System Settings → Privacy &
Security → Open Anyway. Local signing does not provide Apple notarization.

USB enabled. 120 FPS requested; delivered frame rate depends on the session.

Updates: sync your fork, then run Build DMG again.
Source & help: https://github.com/andotsy/quest-vd-mac-usb
